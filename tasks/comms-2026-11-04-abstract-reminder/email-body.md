Hi everyone,

A reminder that abstract submissions for the **2027 CVMBS Research Day** close **Monday, November 16 at 11:59 pm MST** — 12 days from today.

Submit at **researchday.vercel.app**.

### A few things worth clarifying

Because this year's system behaves a little differently than you might expect:

- **Submitting by Nov 16 holds your spot.** You can keep editing your abstract — text, authors, mentors, funding sources — right up until the **January 4** finalization deadline. But a draft that hasn't been *submitted* by Nov 16 doesn't reserve a slot. Click the green **Submit** button, not just **Save Draft**.
- **One abstract per presenter.** Each presenter can only give one talk or poster. If two drafts list the same presenter, only the first one submitted is accepted. Mentors and lab managers submitting on someone's behalf: please use the presenter's real email in the Presenter row.
- **No CSU password.** The site is independent of CSU single-sign-on. You create a free account using any email you check, and a 6-digit login code is emailed to you each time you sign in — the same pattern as Netflix or your bank.
- **Mentors don't need to create the abstract themselves.** If a trainee lists you from the faculty picker, the abstract shows up in *your* account automatically once you've signed up with your `first.last@colostate.edu` alias.

### A little something extra

New for 2027: a live **Research Crawler Log** at **researchday.vercel.app/leaderboard**. It's purely for fun — none of it affects judging — but it is going to make the lead-up to January 23 significantly more interesting.

- **Guild Showdown** and **Department Turnout** rank labs and departments by submission count. Your department is competing right now whether you've noticed or not. Recruit your labmates.
- **Platinum Mentoring Top 10** honors faculty whose trainees show up in force.
- **Green Pipette** rewards abstracts with Green Labs Ambassadors on the author line.
- **Secret achievements** — unlockable "player card" badges. We're not going to tell you what triggers them. You'll know when you earn one. (Submitting early helps.)

So: submit your draft now, nudge your labmates, and check your player card.
