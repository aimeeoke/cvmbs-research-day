# A Submitter's Guide to CVMBS Research Day 2027

**January 23, 2027 · Translational Medicine Institute**
**researchday.vercel.app**

This guide is for anyone submitting an abstract — presenters, trainees, and the mentors or lab managers who draft on someone's behalf. It covers account creation, the two deadlines, and the achievements leaderboard.

---

## The two deadlines (and why they matter)

| Date | What you need to do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday, Nov 16, 2026 — 11:59 pm MST** | Click **Submit** on your draft | Holds your presentation slot. The one-abstract-per-presenter rule locks in based on who's submitted first. |
| **Monday, Jan 4, 2027 — 11:59 pm MST** | Click **Finalize** | The abstract goes to judges and into the printed program. No more edits after this. |

**You can keep editing freely between Nov 16 and Jan 4.** Fix typos, polish results, add or reorder coauthors, swap mentors. The important thing is that **a saved draft is not a submission** — it doesn't hold a slot. Make sure you hit the green **Submit** button by Nov 16.

### One abstract per presenter
Each person can only present one abstract. If two drafts list the same presenter, **only the one that's submitted first is accepted.** If you're a mentor or lab manager submitting on behalf of a student, please type the student's *real* email in the Presenter row so they get the credit.

## Creating your account

1. Go to **researchday.vercel.app** and click **Sign up** (top-right of the landing page, or in the sidebar).
2. Enter your first and last name.
3. Pick your role:
   - **"Presenter, trainee, or non-faculty judge"** — use any email you check often (Gmail, personal, CSU — anything).
   - **"CVMBS Faculty"** — must be your `first.last@colostate.edu` CSU alias (the standard firstname-dot-lastname format that every CVMBS faculty member has, whether or not it's your usual inbox).
4. Click **Create account & send code**. You'll receive a 6-digit code by email within a minute or two.
5. Enter the code on the next screen. You're in — no password to remember.

### Signing in later
Each time you sign in, a new 6-digit code is emailed to you. Think of it like Netflix or your bank: type your email, we send you a code, you type the code back. **You never create a password.**

If a code doesn't arrive within a minute or two, check spam, then click **Resend code**.

> ⚠️ **This site is not CSU single-sign-on.** Don't try to use your eID password — it won't work. We're intentionally outside the university's auth system so community visitors and non-CSU judges can also participate.

## Creating your abstract

1. Sign in, then click **Abstracts** in the sidebar. This is the portal where all your abstracts live.
2. Click **New submission**. A blank draft is created and you're taken to the submit form.
3. Work through the sections in order:
   - **Presenter** — name, email, classification, department or program, affiliations.
   - **Mentors** — a required **Faculty Mentor 1** (pick from the CVMBS faculty list), an optional **Faculty Mentor 2**, and an optional **Other Mentor** for non-CVMBS mentors.
   - **Research type and stage** — basic / clinical / translational, and early-stage vs. advanced-stage.
   - **Title** — supports italics, superscripts, and subscripts. Pasting from Word works and preserves formatting.
   - **Authors** — coauthors in byline order. The site shows you a live byline preview.
   - **Abstract body** — same rich text as the title.
   - **Funding sources, session preference, Green Labs Ambassador certifications** — all at the bottom.
4. **Save Draft** as often as you like. Nothing is final until you click Submit.

### When your draft is ready: click Submit
This is the step that holds your Research Day slot by Nov 16. The site will warn you if the presenter you've listed already has another submitted abstract.

### After Submit (Nov 16 → Jan 4)
Reopen your abstract any time. Text, authors, mentors, funding, session preference — all still editable. The presenter identity is locked in for scheduling purposes, so if you need to change who's presenting, email the committee rather than creating a second draft.

### After Finalize (on or before Jan 4)
Everything locks. If you spot a typo after finalizing, email the committee and we'll take a look.

## The Research Crawler Log — achievements & leaderboard

Separate from the abstract itself, we run a live leaderboard at **researchday.vercel.app/leaderboard**. It's for fun — it doesn't affect judging or award decisions.

What's tracked:

- **Golden Pipette** — top presenter honors.
- **Green Pipette** — Green Labs Ambassador participation (10 points per certified author on your abstract, capped at 100). Check **/green-labs** for the public roster.
- **Platinum Mentoring Top 10** — mentors with the strongest trainee turnout.
- **Guild Showdown** and **Department Turnout** — friendly between-group competition.
- **Secret achievements** — unlockable badges on your personal "player card." Sign in and visit **/leaderboard** to see what you've earned.

If you're a certified My Green Lab Ambassador or a CSU Green Paw participant, you may already be on the roster — the site matches by name against the official lists. If you've completed training and don't see yourself, you can upload your certificate from the submit form (bottom of the authors section).

## If something goes wrong

- **My login code didn't arrive.** Check spam, then click **Resend code**. If it still hasn't arrived after 5 minutes, reply to the committee's reminder email.
- **I can't find my abstract when I log in.** Make sure you're logged in with the same email the submitter typed into the Presenter row. If a mentor or lab manager created the draft, they may have typed a different email than the one you signed up with.
- **The site says the presenter already has a submitted abstract.** That's the one-per-presenter rule in action. Check with your lab, your mentor, or the other person — only the first submission counts.
- **I need to delete a draft.** Go to **Abstracts** → three-dot menu on your row → **Delete**. This works until the abstract is finalized. (Post-finalize, email the committee and we'll handle a withdrawal.)
